08 • 2027 Direction

Take the experience statewide—without making every stop a major production.

A tiered tour model can extend reach while preserving a smaller number of high-impact anchor experiences.

Tier 1

Pop-Up Wellness Stops

Small, nimble activations that can travel to more IU Health locations.

  • Focused service mix
  • Short setup window
  • Shift-friendly hours
  • Portable branding + Passportage
Tier 2

Regional Wellness Days

Mid-sized experiences built around regional employee populations and priority services.

  • Multiple screenings
  • Local ambassadors
  • Appointment support
  • Regional storytelling
Tier 3

Anchor Experiences

A smaller number of larger destination events with the full Your Health Matters footprint.

  • Full partner mix
  • Video + content capture
  • Leadership visibility
  • Flagship measurement
